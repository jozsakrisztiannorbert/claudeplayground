What I film

Wedding films

Full day coverage with a second camera at the ceremony. A film between 5 and 9 minutes, plus a short cut for sharing the same week.

Brand films

One or two shoot days, a story written with you, and cuts sized for the web, Instagram and the screen in your showroom.

Music videos

Performance and narrative pieces for artists who want the song to look the way it sounds. Lighting and locations handled.

Events

Conferences, launches and festivals cut into a recap within days, while people are still talking about it.

How a film happens

  1. Talk

    A call about the day, the people and what has to be in the film. You get a quote and a hold on the date within 48 hours.

  2. Shoot

    I arrive early, work quietly, and leave when the story is over. Two cameras, wireless audio, drone where it is allowed.

  3. Edit and grade

    Picture lock in 6 to 8 weeks for weddings, 2 weeks for brand work. Graded by hand, music licensed, one round of changes included.

  4. Deliver

    A private link, a 4K download that is yours to keep, and the short cut ready for Instagram.
